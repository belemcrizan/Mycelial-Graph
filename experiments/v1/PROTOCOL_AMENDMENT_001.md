# Protocol amendment 001 — enforce evidence integrity before pilot

Date: 2026-09-15 (task date). Baseline: `4966daf3d0f09ba810f6855772145130359eb974`.

## Exposure and purpose

The audit used the checked-in V0/V1 development artifacts, repository source and synthetic regression fixtures. No independent pilot or confirmatory outcomes were collected before these changes. The next real experiment is the original independent 20-seed pilot, after committing the source. This amendment records implementation enforcement and known gaps; it does not change the original protocol or analysis plan.

## Preserved commitments

All original configurations, seed populations, protocol/analysis/schema files, method update and routing equations, scenario noise/shock construction, RNG namespaces, pairing, optimum certification, recovery definition, censoring horizon, percentile bootstrap, primary rho=.50, NI rho=0 with M=.10, one-sided alpha=.05, confidence=.95, power target .80 and point-gain threshold .20 are unchanged. No baseline is added, removed or tuned.

## Enforcement changes

- Validate the entire planned population and byte hashes before inference. Reject missing/unplanned scenarios, duplicate methods, foreign provenance or modified traces.
- Bind fresh executions to source bytes, code revision, config, seed file and protocol snapshots. Prevent concurrent writers, incompatible partial resume and replacement of completed manifests.
- Freeze NumPy buffers and topology maps; remove volatile gzip headers. Numerical outcomes must match the baseline on regression scenarios.
- Creating a seed file alone no longer unlocks confirmatory execution. Require the committed freeze contract, complete reviewed addendum, mechanical first-N seeds, sealed pilot and recorded power review.
- Keep descriptive scientific gates separate from phase eligibility and product promotion. The operational budget in original protocol §8.2 remains unspecified, so automatic promotion is false.
- Report zero pilot variance as `unestimable`; do not choose N or fabricate a power claim.
- Preserve exception diagnostics, including previously completed methods. Do not silently rerun or omit them. Method-vs-infrastructure classification and a complete failure-outcome estimand remain unresolved; automatic inference is suspended on such failures, consistent with analysis plan §6. This is containment, not a new imputation rule.
- Seal raw and processed artifacts after analysis/report. Subsequent analyses require a new versioned artifact.

## Interpretation

No previous result is upgraded. No confirmatory inference is generated by this amendment. New evidence remains development or pilot evidence until all original and strengthened freeze conditions are met. A future measurement, failure-handling or sample-size design change requires a new numbered amendment with its own exposure statement.
