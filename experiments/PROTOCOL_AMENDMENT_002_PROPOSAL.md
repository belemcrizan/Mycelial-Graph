## 1. The decision requested of the human maintainer

The current moratorium (active) freezes V2.0-alpha, V2.1, V2.1-A, `theory/`, `external/`, and the
pooling/real tracks until **Paper A is submitted and its receipt recorded**
(`research/state.json.moratorium`). This proposal asks the maintainer to decide **one narrow
question**:

> May a small, enumerated set of **V1-closure** activities be authorized *before* the moratorium's
> general exit, on the grounds that they *complete the confirmatory science already begun*, rather
> than *expand the program*?

## 2. The distinction this proposal draws

| "Completing the confirmatory science" (proposed as authorizable) | "Expanding the program" (stays frozen) |
|---|---|
| Equalized re-run isolating representation from effective policy scale (WS-1, EQ-B remedy) | V2.0-alpha / V2.1 / V2.1-A capability work |
| Properly-sized ρ=0 safety test (WS-2) | New routing-product / V1.5 feature scope |
| External OPE on OPE-valid logged data (WS-3, Layer 1) | `theory/` complexity-gain claims |
| — | Any new scientific question beyond V1's estimand |

**Argument.** The three left-column items do not ask a new question. They answer the *same* V1
question with (a) the EQ-B confound controlled, (b) the ρ=0 gate properly powered, and (c) a bounded
external check of the recovery phenomenon. Each is *closure of V1*, and each is already prepared as a
frozen, ready-to-authorize artifact:

- `experiments/v1_5/PROTOCOL_EQUALIZED_RERUN.md` + `config.equalized.yaml`
- `experiments/v1/POWER_ANALYSIS_RHO0.md` (properly-sized ρ=0 test, staged)
- `external/protocols/EXTERNAL_OPE_PREREG.md`

## 3. What the amendment would and would not change

**Would (if approved):** narrow the moratorium so the three enumerated V1-closure runs may be
authorized individually, each still subject to its own frozen protocol and its own human "run" flag.

**Would not (hard limits, non-negotiable):**
- It would **not** touch, reopen, requalify, or overwrite the sealed V1 `REFUTED` result — that is
  an interdict, not a gate (MASTER_PROMPT §5.6).
- It would **not** lift the freeze on V2/theory/product tracks.
- It would **not** authorize any paid call or real-user data collection beyond the OPE-valid,
  license-clean logged sources enumerated in the external prereg.
- It would **not** itself execute anything; each run still needs its per-artifact `authorized: true`
  flip, recorded in the ledger.

## 4. Why present it as a human decision

The line between "closure" and "expansion" is a judgment about research scope and integrity that
belongs to the maintainer, not to the agent. The agent's role ends at preparing the proposal and the
frozen artifacts it points to.
