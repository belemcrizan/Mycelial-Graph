# Lower bounds

**OPEN_PROBLEM.** No Mycelial-specific minimax lower bound is claimed.

Relevant classical directions (to be cited only from verified bibliographic records in `research/references.yaml` when used in a paper):

- stationary MAB lower bounds of order $\sqrt{KT}$;
- variation-budget non-stationary bandits;
- sparse versus dense change localization.

**CONJECTURE (informal).** If $\rho=0$ and shocks are edge-wise independent, no sharing method can improve the local minimax rate without additional assumptions that effectively restore sharing.

**ASSUMPTION failure.** If topology is unknown, distinguishing shared from idiosyncratic shocks can require exploration that wipes out the hoped $\sqrt{K_{\mathrm{node}}}$ gain.
