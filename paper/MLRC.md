# ML Reproducibility Challenge

**Checked:** 2026-09-15 against https://reproml.org/ and https://reproml.org/call_for_papers/

**Verdict:** `NOT CURRENTLY AVAILABLE` as a venue for Paper A itself.

MLRC 2026 is open in a narrow sense: it is a NeurIPS 2026 track for *reproducibility papers* that are accepted at TMLR in the window 2025-06-20 to 2026-09-30. Paper A is an original frozen confirmatory experiment, not a reproduction of a previously published paper. It is therefore out of scope as an MLRC submission.

The June 4 2026 expression-of-interest deadline has also passed. Self-nomination of an *accepted* TMLR reproducibility paper remains open until 2026-09-30, which still does not make Paper A an MLRC paper.

## Closest valid external reproduction mechanism

1. Submit Paper A to TMLR with an anonymized reproduction package.
2. After a policy-compatible time, publish the public archive / DOI.
3. Invite independent reproduction using `python reproduce_confirmatory.py`.
4. If a later reproducibility study of Paper A is accepted at TMLR inside a future MLRC window, that study (not Paper A) can be nominated.

Do not call internal CI independent reproduction.
