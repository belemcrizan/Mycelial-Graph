# Do-not-claim registry

Delete a row only when evidence actually supports the claim.

- production readiness
- universal superiority
- causal fault localization
- real-provider generalization
- phase transition
- $\rho^*$ law
- theoretical sample-complexity advantage
- regret improvement as an established theorem
- independent reproduction
- DOI (none deposited)
- provider superiority
- global-scale readiness
- V1 confirmatory recovery advantage
- NeurIPS/ICML acceptance implied by an internal score
- one-command paper reproduction until `make reproduce-paper` exists and works
- live collector measurements (dry-run only)
